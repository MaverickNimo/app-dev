# My Favorite Movie: Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness

> *"Things just got out of hand."* — Doctor Stephen Strange

## Overview
**Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness** (2022) is a Marvel Studios superhero film directed by *Sam Raimi*. Doctor Stephen Strange casts a forbidden spell that opens the doorway to the multiverse, forcing him to team up with old and new mystical allies to face a dangerous adversary.

---

## Why I Love It
1. **Stunning Visual Effects:** The sequence where Strange and America Chavez tumble through dozens of different universes is visually unforgettable.
2. **Compelling Villain:** Wanda Maximoff's transformation into the Scarlet Witch makes her both terrifying and deeply sympathetic.

## Main Characters
* **Dr. Stephen Strange** – Former Sorcerer Supreme protecting reality from mystical threats
* **Wanda Maximoff / Scarlet Witch** – A powerful Avenger corrupted by the *Darkhold*
* **America Chavez** – A teenager with the unique ability to punch star-shaped portals between universes
* **Wong** – The current Sorcerer Supreme of Earth-616
